## 🌟 What Is This Repo?

Welcome to **`violets_homework`**—a custom-engineered, high-energy at-home learning arcade built for **Violet Goresch** (and coached by **Dad Alex & Marisa**)! 

Instead of boring worksheets, this repository transforms Mrs. Molly Ulrich’s **Paris Ridge Elementary 2nd Grade** classroom updates into bite-sized, multisensory **10-to-15 minute Power Sprints**. Featuring Web Speech API audio synthesis, live sound effects, a digital drawing canvas, and a playable Pond-Hopper mini-game, Violet earns **Champion Coins 🪙** and **Arena Stars ⭐** to unlock **30+ minutes of free creative time!**

## 🕹️ Inside the 5 Championship Game Show Arenas

### 🚦 Stage 0: Pre-Sprint MTSS Champion Ritual
Before the sprint timer starts, Violet completes her **3-Step Champion Check** to earn her first **+50 Champion Coins**:
1. 🚰 **Clear the Deck:** Bathroom break done, water bottle ready, 1 sharp pencil + scratch paper at the desk.
2. 🧘‍♀️ **Body Ready to Learn:** Feet grounded, shoulder blades relaxed, standing counter or movement stool ready.
3. 👀 **Eyes on Speaker:** Soft eye contact with Dad or Marisa during mission briefings.
4. 🗣️ **Repeat the Task:** Violet repeats the sprint goal back in her own words before touching the keyboard or pencil!

---

### 🥋 Arena 1: Tadpole-to-Frog Compound Word Karate Chop *(Sprint 1 • 5 Mins)*
Half of this week's spelling words are 7-to-8 letter compound words! Violet uses a virtual **Karate Chop** to split each word right between its two base roots, arm-taps each syllable (**Shoulder $\rightarrow$ Elbow $\rightarrow$ Wrist**), spots the hidden Long `/o/` pattern, and fuses them back together!
* 💡 **Mrs. Ulrich's Golden Rule:** Remember that the `/k/` sound at the start of a root word (like `coat` in `raincoat`) almost always starts with **C** instead of K!

---

### 🔐 Arena 2: The 5 Secret Long `/O/` Vaults Detective Sort *(Sprint 1 • 5 Mins)*
A 3-in-1 phonics detective laboratory with full text-to-speech audio soundboards:
* **Mode 1 — Vault Sorter:** Sort all 10 weekly words into their 5 secret Long `/o/` pattern vaults.
* **Mode 2 — Mystery Letter Locks:** Crack the 10 vault locks by picking the missing vowel team (`o`, `o_e`, `oa`, `ow`, `oe`).
* **Mode 3 — Live Audio Spelling Test:** Hear each word in a game-show sentence and type it out with non-spoiler arm-tap hints!

| Vault 1: Open `o` | Vault 2: Magic `o_e` | Vault 3: Team `oa` | Vault 4: Team `ow` | Vault 5: Team `oe` |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| *End of 1st syllable* | *Silent 'e' jumps over* | *Walking vowel team* | *End of root/syllable* | *Tricky end team* |
| **mo•ment** | **tad•pole** | **oat•meal** | **snow•ball** | **toe•nail** |
| **fro•zen** | **ex•plode** | **rain•coat** | **el•bow** | **goes** |

### 🦋 Arena 3: Metamorphosis Boss-Level 5-Finger Retell & Pond Leap *(Movement Break • 5 Mins)*
Bridges active physical movement between academic sprints with Mrs. Ulrich's **Cycles of Nature** unit!
* **🖐️ 5-Finger Temporal Retell:** Sequence and act out the 5 stages of a **Frog** (or **Butterfly**) across the living room rug using temporal transition words:
  * **Finger 1 (Thumb) — FIRST:** 🥚 Curl up tight into a tiny **Egg** on the rug!
  * **Finger 2 (Pointer) — THEN:** 〰️🐸 Hatch and wiggle around like a hungry **Tadpole**!
  * **Finger 3 (Middle) — NEXT:** 🦵🐸 Grow strong back legs into a **Pollywog**!
  * **Finger 4 (Ring) — THEN:** ✨🐸 **Metamorphosis!** Grow front legs and shrink your tail as a **Froglet**!
  * **Finger 5 (Pinky) — LAST:** 🐸👑 Leap big across the room as an adult **Frog**!
* **🎮 Bonus Arcade Mini-Game:** Play **Tadpole-to-Frog Pond Leap** using arrow keys or on-screen buttons to hop across lily pads, catch glowing Long `/o/` bubbles, and evolve through all 5 stages!

---

### 🎙️ Arena 4: *Kids Excel* Sportscaster Studio & Gratitude Mic Drop *(Sprint 2 • 15 Mins)*
Combines classroom reading fluency (*Kids Excel*) with **quotation mark (`" "`) mechanics** and November's SEL Gratitude theme:
* **Pass 1 (Warm-Up Lap):** Read the teleprompter script focusing on accuracy and arm-tapping tricky vowel teams.
* **Pass 2 (Live TV Broadcast):** Hit the **On-Air Chime 🔴**, grab a microphone, and reread with huge sportscaster expression whenever encountering glowing `"quotation marks"`!
* **Pass 3 (Gratitude Mic Drop & Drawing Pad):** Write a gratitude sentence using at least one Long `/o/` word and quotation marks (auto-checked by the live **3-Star Mechanics Detective**), then illustrate the scene on the built-in HTML5 drawing canvas!

---

### 💥 Arena 5: The "Explode" the Number Lab & Tadpole Hops *(Sprint 2 • 15 Mins)*
Three interactive math stations aligned with Mrs. Ulrich's classroom strategies:

#### ⚡ Part A: 3-Minute Staged Flashcard Sprint
* **Confident Base Pile:** Teens, $+0, +1, +2, +3$, and Doubles.
* **This Week's Challenger Pile:** **$+4$ Facts** ($6+4, 7+4, 8+4, 9+4$) with interactive Ten-Frame visualizers to beat finger-counting!

#### 💥 Part B: "Explode" Double-Digit Expanded Addition
Break apart two-digit addends into Tens and Ones, add them separately, and regroup $10$ ones into a new ten:
$$\text{Classroom Model: } 58 + 42 = (50 + 8) + (40 + 2) = (50 + 40) + (8 + 2) = 90 + 10 = 100$$

#### 🐸 Part C: Part-Part-Whole Detective & Open Number Line Tadpole Hops
1. **Rule 1 (Task Retell First):** Identify the **WHOLE** ($53$ snowballs) and the **PART** hopping away ($23$ snowballs) before touching the number line.
2. **Rule 2 (Hop to a Friendly $10$):** Start at $53 \xrightarrow{-3} 50 \xrightarrow{-10} 40 \xrightarrow{-10} 30$!

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Zero-Dependency Architecture

* **Single-File HTML5 Engine (`index.html`):** 100% self-contained HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript—zero build steps or external frameworks required.
* **Web Speech API (`window.speechSynthesis`):** Real-time pronunciation of spelling words, syllables, and sportscaster sentences.
* **Web Audio API Synthesizer:** Custom-generated arcade chimes, karate-chop whooshes, vault unlocks, and victory fanfare with a toggleable mute switch.
* **HTML5 Canvas (`<canvas>`):** Touch- and stylus-friendly Gratitude Drawing Pad + interactive Pond Hopper arcade mini-game.
* **LocalStorage Persistence:** Automatically saves Violet's Champion Coins, Arena Stars, streak counter, and Monday–Thursday parent sign-off logs between sessions.
* **Automated CI/CD (`.github/workflows/static.yml`):** Instant deployment to GitHub Pages on every push to `main`.
